# BloodLinq

**Connecting people who need blood with people willing to give it.**

BloodLinq is a mobile app that helps patients, families and hospitals find blood donors quickly. Donors register with their blood group and area. When someone needs blood, they can search for nearby donors or post a request, and matching donors close by are notified so they can respond.

## Why BloodLinq

Finding blood in an emergency often means calling around, posting in group chats and waiting. BloodLinq puts willing donors and people in need in one place, grouped by location, so help is found faster.

## Who it's for

- **Donors**: people who want to be reachable when someone nearby needs their blood group.
- **Receivers**: patients, relatives and hospitals looking for a donor.

One account can do both. Anyone can request blood, and anyone can choose to become a donor.

## What it does

- Lets people register as blood donors
- Groups donors by area so receivers find help nearby
- Lets receivers search for donors or post blood requests
- Connects donors and receivers directly by call or WhatsApp

## A note on safety

BloodLinq connects people. It does not test or screen blood. Donations should always be made through a hospital or licensed blood bank.
